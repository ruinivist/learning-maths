## Guess Birthday Problem

Boss A’s birthday is one of these 10 dates:

- March: 4, 5, 8
- June: 4, 7
- September: 1, 5
- December: 1, 2, 8

A tells you only the month and your colleague C only the day.

The conversation goes:

1. You: “I don’t know A’s birthday, and C doesn’t know either.”
2. C: “I didn’t know before, but now I know.”
3. You: “Now I know too.”

Question: Based on this conversation, what is A’s birthday?

## Solution

The core idea is to realise that that if I know the day but am not sure then the day must be repeated.

From there it's easy to make groups and follow along.
