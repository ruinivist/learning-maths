# Equal Card Game

A full deck 0f 52. You flip two, if both red then 1 point to you, if both black, 1 to dealer.
Any other case is discarded.
I win if I score **more**, what is probability of winning?

## Solution

If you take two red out, probab of it being black increases. Since we start at equals, the numbers
of R or B in the stack will ALWAYS be the same, where they decrease they both decrease by one.
So in the end, both will have SAME number of cards.

A tie always, hence can never win.
