# Quant salary

8 quant want to know the avergae salary for the group but don't want anyone to know any other person's salary.
How can we do so?

## Solution

Any reversible operation like add or xor will work.
The first person will take his salary, add something random to it and pass on to the rest who add their own salaries.

At the end, the first person subtracts and calculates the average.
