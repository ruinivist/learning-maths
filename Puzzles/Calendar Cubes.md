# Calendar Cubes

You can assign custom single-digit numbers to the faces of two dice. Can you use them to display every date from 01 to 31?

## Solution

It's a silly problem.
Since 11 and 22 repeat a digit, both dice must have 1 and 2.
Put 0 on one die so you can show 01.

Now the first die has three faces left and the second has four. One 0 isn't enough to show every date from 01 to 09, so put a 0 on the second die too.

Both dice now have 0, 1, and 2. The remaining digits are 3 through 9, but only six faces are left. Use one face for both 6 and 9 by turning it upside down, then place the other digits on the remaining faces.
