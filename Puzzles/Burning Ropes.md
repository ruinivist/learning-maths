# Burning Rope

Assumptions:

- Each rope take 60 minutes to burn
- Unequal burning so you can't use length
- Can ignite only at ends, one or boths,

## 30 mins or 60 mins

Both are trivial just one rope will do, light both ends for 30 and one for 60.

## 45 mins

Light both ends for one rope ( above) and just one for the second. From the first one, you measure 30 mins, the other rope has to have 30 mins left in it. Light the other end to measure 15 then.

## 15 mins

Same as 45 mins, if you start timing when you light the 2nd end of rope B.

## 90 mins

Sequentially, 30 mins using first rope and 60 using second.

## 75 mins and 3 ropes?

You can start to merge sequential subproblems. Measure 45 using 2 and then 30 using 1.
