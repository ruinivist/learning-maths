This repository contains my maths learning notes. Most tasks involve fixing LaTeX.

- Use dollar-based KaTeX syntax: `$...$` for inline maths and `$$...$$` for display maths, as appropriate.
- Keep equations together. Avoid unnecessary line breaks, especially at equals signs.
- Keep the focus on the maths. Define terms and symbols, but keep explanations brief and avoid long paragraphs.
- Always use the repo-local [prose-humanizer skill](.agents/skills/prose-humanizer/SKILL.md) to keep writing clear and concise.
